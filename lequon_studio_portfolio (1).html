[ 01 / 05 · THE PRACTICE ]

Small by conviction. Senior in execution. Every project is designed and built by the same pair of hands.

Most agencies separate thinking from making. Strategy happens in one room, design in another, and development is handed off at the end. Along the way, the quiet nuances that make a brand feel unmistakable get lost in translation.

I founded Lequon Studio in 2018 as an antidote to bloated creative rosters. By keeping the practice intentionally a studio of one, clients work directly with the person shaping their identity, writing the grid, and building the production site.

Trained in Swiss typographic systems and Japanese editorial print production, my approach balances structural clarity with tactile warmth. Whether designing a custom wordmark, a cloth-bound monograph, or a Webflow flagship, the goal remains identical: longevity over novelty.

When a project requires specialized 3D, industrial hardware engineering, or copywriting, I assemble a bespoke circle of senior independent collaborators—never junior intermediaries.

ETHOS

Three rules that govern the work.

[ PRINCIPLE / 01 ]

Clarity is the highest form of craft.

If an interface requires explanation or an identity relies on a passing visual trend to feel relevant, the structure isn't finished yet. We reduce until only the essential remains.

REDUCTION · STRUCTURE
[ PRINCIPLE / 02 ]

Typography carries 80% of the voice.

Before color, motion, or photography, how a brand sets its words defines its authority. Every project begins with rigorous typographic scale, optical kerning, and grid tension.

PROPORTION · CADENCE
[ PRINCIPLE / 03 ]

Built for the fifth year, not launch day.

Launch announcements fade in 48 hours. A well-architected CMS and modular identity system continue compounding commercial trust years after handover.

ENDURANCE · AUTONOMY
